The Author

Twenty years in.
Still learning. Still growing.

I have been a woman in ministry for over two decades — and this space is everything I wished existed when I started. The honest conversations about leadership, belonging, faith, and what it means to be a woman who follows God in the middle of a real, complicated life.

Psalm 23 Woman is rooted in a theology I call Garden Living — the belief that God's original design for us was wholeness, presence, and rest. This site is an invitation back to that.
